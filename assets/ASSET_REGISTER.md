# Asset register

The playable Primary Controls course uses project-created geometry and audio. The selection screens use seven generated illustrations inspired by the user's visual references. Those illustrations are concept art for the menu, not gameplay screenshots. No third-party asset pack has been imported. Candidate libraries in `PROTOTYPE_PLAN.md` remain research leads.

| Asset | Creator and source | License / use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sedan exterior and cockpit | Maniubra prototype, `scripts/sedan.gd` | Original project work | Procedural Godot meshes/materials, including full roof shell, headliner, windshield header, matching side-door shells and trim, transparent side panes, externally mounted rearward driver-facing mirrors with narrow front pillars, wheel, lower compact dashboard gauges, a gear label attached to the shift knob, seats, hood, hand-over-hand steering motion, shift/handbrake/seatbelt reaches, and a moving handbrake lever beside the steering wheel. Primary Controls adds original ribbed animated pedals and an ignition button, with lesson-only emissive highlights and a live control close-up. The [user-provided driving-school clip](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1omn7NdF0nc) was a motion reference; no video frames or assets were incorporated. |
| Iloilo district blockout | Maniubra prototype, `scripts/world/iloilo_district.gd` | Original project work | Procedural roads, bridge crossings, sidewalks, plaza/church silhouette, venue bay, signage and riverside geometry. Existing original shopfront, palm and stall scenes are reused. Landmarks are stylized blockouts pending a reference-based art pass. |
| Trailer Scenario market | Maniubra prototype, `scripts/world/trailer_market.gd` | Original project work | Fictional Iloilo-inspired palengke: rounded two-lane loop, twelve produce/food stalls, market hall and gateway, corrugated roofs, tarpaulins, utility wires, laundry, sacks, carts and roadworks. Reuses original shopfront, palm, cone and tricycle scenes. All meshes and signs are authored in Godot; no external assets were imported. |
